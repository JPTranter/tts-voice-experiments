The colony ship Argent drifted past the terminator line, its hull ticking as forty years of micrometeorites gave up their heat.

Inside, Mira watched the planet's dawn crawl across the viewport — a world of rust and ice that no human had ever touched.

"We came expecting a home," she said. "Instead we found a question."

Behind her, the ship's mind answered in a voice like falling snow: "Then we shall spend our lives learning how to ask it properly."
